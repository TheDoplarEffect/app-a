## Notes

- My lens confirmed the UX report's coverage note: all interactive targets already meet 48dp (CircleIconButton, buttons, nav cells, switch, search clear, banner) and destructive actions have confirm dialogs + toasts — no change needed there; the real UX defects are the re-animation flicker (#36-38), reduced-motion (#39), form keyboard/a11y (#40-42), and accessible dynamic state (#34-35).
- The single biggest user-visible item in my focus area is #30 (contrast validated against a backdrop that never exists on screen) together with #29 (poster dead zone) — both are WCAG 1.4.3 legibility failures on text the user actually reads (11-14sp labels, status pills).
- Several "HIGH" findings from other lenses (poller pile-up #1, main-thread persist #6, post-destroy polling #7) are also UX-relevant as jank/battery drains, so I approved them; pure refactors that only reorganize code without changing anything the user sees (#19-27, #49-63) I rejected per the small-high-impact rule.
- Items #4 and #18 are the small, user-visible cores of architecture's big SSOT findings — approving those two captures the actual bug (placeholder data shown as live; statuses frozen after cold start) without the package-split refactor.
